What am I seeing?
WISE 0855−0714 is one of the coldest known sub-stellar objects, situated just 7.4 light-years away. With a surface temperature around −23 °C (250 K)—colder than Antarctic ice—it emits almost exclusively in the thermal infrared.

In Difference Mode, subtracting the first epoch (2025-05-05) from the latest (2026-05-02) cancels static background stars, isolating the brown dwarf as a distinct bright-dark dipole. The 4 numbered cyan markers confirm its observed shift matches the catalog proper motion vector (μ ≈ 8.1″/yr).
